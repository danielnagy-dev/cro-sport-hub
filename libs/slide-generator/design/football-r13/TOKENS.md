## Grid

- Player, no hero (balanced): 1 → 1 row, 2 → 2, 3 → 3, 4 → 2+2, 5 → 3+2, 6 → 3+3, 7 → 4+3, 8 → 4+4
- Player with hero: hero column 1.3fr spanning 2 rows + remaining stats in 2 rows (cols = ceil(n/2), max 4)
- List: up to 4 columns of 104px (hero column 128px); column count = max needed by any row on the slide; left aligned
- Dividers: 1px rule, inner only (no left border on first column, no top border on first row), no outer frame, no cell backgrounds

## Radii

- 0 px everywhere. Square everything: no rounded corners on cells, markers or brand mark. Only round shapes are the passes donut ring (circle) and stroke caps of icons.

## Icons

- 24 px grid, stroke 1.75, round caps/joins, `currentColor`; files in `src/icons/` (project-drawn, no third-party licence).
